Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI engine powers my presentations?

The Free Starter plan runs on NVIDIA NIM endpoints (Llama 3.1 70B Instruct, Llama 3.3 70B, and Mixtral 8x7B) to autonomously craft slide structure, bullet arguments, and speaker notes. The $8/month Pro Plan (Coming Soon) upgrades the synthesis engine to Claude Sonnet 5.5 for deeper reasoning, sharper narrative arcs, and more nuanced financial storytelling.

What is Claude Sonnet 5.5 and why does Pro use it?

Claude Sonnet 5.5 is the frontier reasoning model powering the Pro Plan. It delivers materially better long-context coherence across full decks, stronger quantitative analysis for board metrics, and more precise adherence to your corporate brand voice than open-weights models alone.

Can I export directly to Microsoft PowerPoint?

Yes! GlowSlides includes native PPTX compilation. You can download real, fully editable PowerPoint (.pptx) decks, standalone interactive HTML files, or Markdown outlines with a single click.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

Your decks live in your private, encrypted workspace and are only visible to you. Nothing you generate is ever used to train AI models, and you can permanently delete any presentation at any time.
